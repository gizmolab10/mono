# truth and zone — divide them further?

29 August 2026. Three ideas weighed in one sitting. The full pros and cons are in shared decisions, under Evaluations; this is the review copy.

## 1. subfolders under truth/ and zone/

Not yet. A flat truth/ is the whole catalog at one glance, and index.md already does the grouping a subfolder would. The sizing caps are the design's pressure to merge and delete; subfolders relieve exactly that pressure. Today shared/truth holds 7 files and ov/truth 5 — half the ~15 cap. The design's own rule: split when a cap is exceeded, not before.

## 2. a design/ folder beside truth/

Hinges on which taxonomy governs. The code splits guide-from-design (`is_design`, notes/designs/); the memory design splits is-from-thinking, and under that reading a current design is simply a truth — controls.md is `type: design` and lives in truth/ comfortably. A third folder would make every "where" a coin flip between two doors. Only the code's taxonomy winning would justify it.

## 3. is divides into ai vs app; thinking needs no divide

The divide is real: sorting today's files gives almost no coin flips — protocol, conventions, lexicon, the howto are ai; controls, scope, claude-files are app. Two strays: decisions.md (holds both kinds) and taste (app-flavored, lives in shared).

Then the optimization — equal counts on each side of a cut — decides where the divide belongs. It already exists as **shared vs project**: shared's truths are the ai side (six), the projects' truths are the app side (ov five, lv two), roughly even. Cutting inside a folder instead would split shared 6-to-1 and ov 5-to-0 — the worst counts a divide can have.

## where this leaves things

No new folders. The ai/app divide is the structure already in place; a subfolder earns its way only when one folder's own pile grows past its cap AND splits near its middle. The two strays wait: decisions.md mixing both kinds, and taste's home.
