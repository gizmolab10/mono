# Write a plan

Rules for a plan file, distilled 15 September 2026 from the plan for kb, mu and ai in memory/ai/zone/work.

1. One list, numbered through, so the next step is the line below. Steps and substeps, never lists naming one another by number.
2. A question a step needs is a substep right before the work, opening with question in bold. An answer goes where its question is: the question's line becomes the statement, and the question goes.
3. Gather what is scattered into one section, then remove what became redundant.
4. One idea per bullet, nested under the thing it details.
5. A step opens with the issue, then the fix, then what the fix makes.
6. A risk is stated as what the step avoids, not as a level the step wears. A risk not yet present names its when: "not edgy until steps 26 and 29."
7. Say what is, never what would have been. The road not taken lives in decisions.md.
