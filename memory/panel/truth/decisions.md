# Decisions

## Evaluations (pac) made during 2026

- 9 September 2026 — **does panel remain, a template with no app of its own, now that mu and mj hold their own versions of its files?** What exists: panel is a project in both halves. In mono, four components, a Core.ts, a Customizations.ts holding one switch, an entry file, and five registrations outside its folder — ports.json at 5186, mono's workspaces, servers.sh, and three lines in the hub under F. In memory, an index, a log, an empty ideas file and this one question. It draws the three regions, empty, and nothing else. mu and mj took its four files on 7 September, each as its own, and both have since changed theirs: the name label was ported to all three by hand, and each operation view holds a line of its own. incorporating a project.md, step 3, names panel as the place a host takes the four files from. Two undecided pieces name it: the Importing proposal, where panel becomes a library hosts import, and the library pac in shared's decisions, where panel is one of three. For keeping it: it is the one clean version, the source a fourth host would take from, and the proving ground for a change to the four files before mu or mj carry it. The Importing proposal needs it to exist, since a library must live somewhere, and the library pac counts it. Its cost today is a hub button that opens three empty regions. Against keeping it: it is a third version of the same 200 lines, and the only one nobody runs — every change to the four files is now made in mu or mj and ported to panel by hand, or not. A host has already taken from it, so the next one could take from mu. A project with no purpose beyond being taken from has nothing to log, nothing to decide and nothing to build, and its memory shows it: one question, no ideas. A middle path: panel remains until the Importing proposal is decided, and that decision decides this one — imported, it becomes the library and stays; not imported, it goes, and incorporating a project.md takes its files from mu. Cost, counted: to remove, five registrations, two folders, one step rewritten in incorporating a project.md, two mentions amended in the Importing proposal and the library pac. To keep, nothing today, and one port by hand for every change a host makes to the four files. Deciding question: is panel a source hosts take files from, kept in step by hand, or a library hosts import, or nothing now that two hosts hold its files — and if nothing, where does step 3 take the four files from? Decided 10 September 2026: a library hosts import. Panel.svelte is the page as a component, and gallery, mj and mu import it; mj's and mu's own versions of the region files are deleted, so nobody holds a version but panel.
