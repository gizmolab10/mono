# Hello — this is just the beginning

Welcome to Intersection. It's a young app, and you're early.

Intersection keeps your documents in the browser and, on the **AI** store, mirrors
their words to a private helper you can ask questions of. Drop files or whole folders,
tag them, look through them, and — on the AI store — chat with what you've gathered.

## The quick tour

- **The pill at the top** switches what the middle of the screen shows: your list of
  documents, the drop box for adding more, or (on the AI store) the chat.
- **The hamburger** at the top-left opens the side details: the accent color, and the
  data readout with its store switcher.
- **The store switcher** (in the details, under *more*) moves between your own files and
  the shared AI.

## Adding documents

Drop files or folders anywhere on the list, or open the drop box from the pill. Big
files are kept whole; a folder brings its whole tree.

## Asking the AI

On the AI store, the **chat** shows a running conversation — your question, then the
answer, and the documents it drew from. A refresh keeps the conversation.

See **what's broken** for the rough edges that are still being smoothed.
