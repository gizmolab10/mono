## Pitch for Dr. Aaron Good
  
Hello Aaron,  
  
Many of us have followed your work with deep appreciation for your ability to piece together fragments—connecting dots across disparate sources to help us understand assassinations and state crimes that the system has deliberately obscured. This is no simple task. Few know as well as you that the evidence is there, but it is scattered across decades, locked in archives, buried in declassified documents, and spread across the work of scholars who have been systematically marginalized.  
  
My brother and I are building a tool [intended] to solve that dilemma, and we would like to invite you to our pilot application to see if it can assist you in your work.  
  
It is a private, AI-powered research assistant that allows you to upload your own work—alongside the work of [colleagues like] Peter Dale Scott and Lance deHaven-Smith—and query it for connections that [can] take years to find manually. Ask it something like "What patterns connect the assassination of JFK, the Iran-Contra affair, and the CIA's involvement in the 1953 Iranian coup?" and it retrieves relevant passages, cites sources, and surfaces links you might have missed.  
  
[Our] immediate goal is a functional proof of concept: a closed, invite-only research assistant for a small group of founding scholars. We provide the AI infrastructure; you provide the data. But the deeper purpose is preservation and prosecution. We [want to create] a place to [gather and preserve] the legacy of knowledge [written] by scholars like Scott and deHaven-Smith. [Our ultimate goal is] to ensure their work is not lost, but [thrives in a living trust. One that is easily searched and cross-referenced. That] knowledge then needs to be weaponized not just for understanding, but [indeed] for action.


  
[We are interested in the potential that this platform has] to evolve into a public-facing civic tool—one that pairs journalism with vetted solutions and transforms public solidarity into measurable political leverage. But that is downstream. The first milestone is a rigorous, useful research assistant for the people who do this work.  
  
[So, if we can eventually tailor our AI engine to elevate your momentum, we have an invitation. Our AI will have a "Prime Directive," providing it a strong ethical framework. It is not yet built. Would you like to contribute to it's definition and perhaps its development as a public treasure?]
  
In short: a useful research [and library tool for teams of serious people, helping to preserve] the intellectual legacy of the scholars who came before us and laying the groundwork for a population equipped with the evidence to take control of their world.  
  
I would love to walk you through the prototype.
